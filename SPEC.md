# Ray|Works

Ray|Works is a collection of enterprise apps built on top of Microsoft Rayfin (Fabric Apps) platform.

## Apps

- Ray|Forms: A form builder for creating and managing enterprise forms.
- Ray|Live: A live polling and Q&A app for enterprise meetings and events.
- Ray|Deck: A presentation tool for creating and managing enterprise presentations.
- Ray|Trip: A travel management app for enterprise employees.
- Ray|Stickers: A sticker creation app for enterprise communication.
- Ray|Burn: An AI-powered meeting and outcome cost calculator for enterprise meetings.
